### 1. 100m Ochre Dust Signature Reads

At 100-meter visibility in heavy ochre particulate, fine detail is dead. The driver’s eye registers only **macro-massing silhouettes**, **dominant luminance planes**, and **scatter wavelength**. 

```
[ SKY / CEILING ]
Daikoku:   Skeletal cross-trusses & counterweights (cut against sodium backscatter)
Tenjin:    Parallel sheer vertical slabs; total sky occlusion; elevator cable verticals
Kotobuki:  Continuous low concrete ceiling (expressway deck) crushing down to 4.5m

[ EYE-LEVEL CHANNEL ]
Chidori:   Dense, rhythmic vertical blade-sign staggered slashes (crimson/amber)
Shinkai:   Overhead high-pressure pipe bundles; rhythmic gas flare blooms (sulfur/black)

[ GROUND PLANE ]
Kamome:    Specular wet-floor bounce; low horizontal fluorescent white sheets
```

#### Daikoku Piers — Skeletal Industrial Trussing & Orange Backscatter
* **Silhouette/Massing:** Angular overhead skeletal gantry rails and A-frame crane counterweights towering above shipping container canyons stacked strictly 4-high. 
* **Luminance:** Unshielded, high-pressure sodium floodlights mounted at 30-meter elevation point down into the dust, creating massive cone-shaped volumetric light-shafts that silhouette the rust-orange steel latticework.

#### Kamome Wholesale Ward — Ground-Plane Horizontal White Bloom
* **Silhouette/Massing:** Squat, low-clearance eaves made of corrugated fiberglass, broken only by dripping ice-melt drains. 
* **Luminance:** Zero direct sky view. The signature is entirely on the horizontal ground plane: glaring 4000K cool-white fluorescent strips, hung beneath eaves, bounce off perpetually flooded, fish-scale-coated roadway tarmac. The road glows wet tile-white beneath the ochre air.

#### Chidori Neon Strip — Staggered Vertical Blade Occlusion
* **Silhouette/Massing:** Claustrophobic vertical trench where building faces are stepped backward, choked with multi-tiered steel catwalks and fire escapes.
* **Luminance:** The only district using concentrated gas-discharge lighting. Saturated red and deep amber vertical blade signs project 2 meters out from building facades at alternating heights every 10 meters, creating a repeating physical slalom of warm color bars cutting through the haze.

#### Tenjin Mid-Corridor — The Brutalist Concrete Canyon
* **Silhouette/Massing:** Unbroken, monolithic verticality. Sheer 90-degree concrete slabs with zero lateral fire escapes or external pipework. Massive 4-inch vertical bronze expansion seams and the tensioned steel track cables of the Sky-Elevator Terminal 2 plunge straight into the asphalt.
* **Luminance:** Dark architecture with floor-level upward bronze wash-lighting grazing the concrete, forming two parallel vertical light-sheets flanking the roadway.

#### Kotobuki Slums & Repair Row — The Low-Ceiling Under-Deck
* **Silhouette/Massing:** A crushing, flat horizontal "roof" formed by the underside of the multi-lane elevated municipal expressway. The road channel is hemmed in by massive cylindrical concrete bridge piers wrapped in rusted rebar jackets.
* **Luminance:** Dingy tin-blue. The undersides of the concrete decks catch intermittent, intense bursts of cobalt and cold blue-white light from open-bay submerged arc welding and cutting torches inside chop-shop stalls.

#### Shinkai Basin — Over-Pipe Labyrinths & Flare Blooms
* **Silhouette/Massing:** A tangle of insulated high-pressure process pipelines spanning the street overhead at varying clearances (some sagging dangerously low to 3.8m), broken by massive spherical cracking vats.
* **Luminance:** Permanent, choking sulfur-yellow haze punctuated by the dull, rhythmic percussive flash of rooftop flare stacks burning off unrefined Sol-distillate. The sky doesn't fade to black; it roils in muddy, toxic yellow.

### 2. Translating *Tekkonkinkreet* Density to a Machine-Trench Street

To replicate the dense, claustrophobic hand-drawn clutter of *Tekkonkinkreet* without dynamic pedestrian systems or expensive draw-calls:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------+
| [AERIAL] Drooping cable looms, pneumatic transit tubes    |
|          and dead transformers at windshield level        |
|                                                           |
| [MID-WALL] Layered enamel inspection plates & tax stamps  |
|            stacked 10-deep over utility junction boxes   |
|                                                           |
| [PERCHES] Static masked sentinels leaning on railings;   |
|           filter masks, heavy canvas, staring down        |
|                                                           |
| [CURB]    Windrows of rubber scrub, oil-drum crash barriers|
+-----------------------------------------------------------+
```

#### Aerial Utility Choke (The Low Ceiling)
* **Prop Treatment:** String bundled power lines, phone pairs, and 4-inch canvas-wrapped pneumatic transit tubes directly across the roadway between buildings. 
* **The Trick:** Hang them low—just clearing cab roof-racks (3.2m to 4m). Attach heavy cast-iron transformer cans, dead porcelain insulators, and counterweight pulleys that sag into the upper third of the player's windshield view. This physically frames and compresses the drivable trench.

#### Clustered Stamped-Steel & Enamel Sign Atlases
* **Decal Treatment:** Instead of single signs, use dense composite decal atlases representing decades of municipal bureaucracy. 
* **The Trick:** Place clusters of 6 to 15 small, stamped-steel enamel plates over every utility junction box, lamppost, and bridge abutment: municipal ward tax tags, Sol-fuel rationing certificates, high-voltage warnings, and filter-change maintenance dates. Stack them slightly overlapping to create an intensely detailed, weathered physical paper/metal trail of civic function.

#### The "Observer" Perch (Static Human Presence)
* **Static Character Placement:** Drivers are alone, but the city is watching. Place static, low-poly masked figures strictly out of vehicular reach: perched on exterior fire escapes, behind reinforced glass booth slits, or leaning over catwalk railings.
* **The Costume Law:** Heavy rubberized civil-defense coats, canvas aprons, and vulcanized rubber respirators with external dual-canister filters. They do not walk. They lean on railings, arms crossed, or hold clipboards, their mask lenses catching headlights as you barrel underneath.

#### Curbside "Machine Slag" Windrows
* **Prop Treatment:** Streets must never meet walls at clean 90-degree angles. Fill the gutter line with physical detritus: stacks of dented 20-liter kerosene tins, wire-tied bundles of soaked corrugated cardboard, dead lead-acid battery casings, blown bias-ply truck retreads, and ochre dust piled up into concrete-hard windrows by mechanical street-sweepers that gave up halfway.

### 3. Dust-City Drive-Up Infrastructure

Because drivers cannot step out into the toxic, lung-shredding ochre air without full gear, city operations run entirely driver-to-infrastructure.

```
       [ HIGH-PRESSURE OVERHEAD BOOM ]
                     |
       +-------------v-------------+
       |   FILTER-RINSE BLOWDOWN   | -> Purges dry-filter cakes
       +---------------------------+    without stopping engine
                     |
+--------------------v--------------------+
|  PNEUMATIC TUBE POST    GUILLOTINE SILL |
|  - Brass screw-caps     - Laminated drop|
|  - Vacuum dispatch        counterweight |
+-----------------------------------------+
```

#### 1. Filter-Rinse Air-Lance Boom (Overhead Drive-Through Bay)
* **Form & Function:** Mounted to curbside structural columns at municipal pull-offs. An articulated, counterbalanced yellow steel swing-arm hangs down to window height, terminating in a high-pressure pneumatic blast-cone and solvent trigger. 
* **The Interaction:** Drivers pull up under the boom, crack their storm-vent, grab the lance, and blast their front-fender air-intakes with compressed air and kerosene vapor to blow out dry ochre dust-caking without killing the engine or unclamping the intake housing. 
* **World Detail:** The pavement below is a stained, grease-blackened trough sunken with iron drain gratings choked with orange mud.

#### 2. Cast-Iron Curbside Pneumatic Dispatch Pedestals
* **Form & Function:** Bolted directly into the curb at delivery destinations, chop-shops, and toll checkpoints. Heavy, rust-pitted cast-iron pedestals terminating in an angled, waist-high brass receiving hatch with a heavy turn-wheel lock.
* **The Interaction:** The driver unscrews an aluminum or brass pneumatic carrier cylinder, drops their manifest, fuel chits, or route token inside, slams the hatch shut, and pulls a mechanical steel trip-lever. 
* **World Detail:** A high-pitched vacuum shriek echoes through external iron piping running up the building face. An analog, vacuum-tube-illuminated green/red indicator lens tells the driver if the line holds pressure or if the depot tube is choked with dust.

#### 3. Counter-Weighted "Guillotine" Canteen Sills
* **Form & Function:** Drive-up nutrient and supply windows for drivers who cannot leave their cabs. The stall is completely sealed behind 2-inch thick, dust-pitted laminated glass.
* **The Interaction:** A heavy, canvas-gasketed steel guillotine hatch hangs balanced on thick lead counterweights. When the masked cook steps on an internal foot treadle, the hatch drops open just 8 inches. 
* **World Detail:** A greased sheet-steel tray slides forward across the sill on ball bearings, carrying a lidded tin of hot broth, replacement respirator cartridges, and loose tobacco. The transaction is conducted by sliding stamped brass currency coins into a slot cut into the steel sill; no hand contact, no ambient dust ingress.

#### 4. Overhead Sol-Fuel Gravity Dispenser Towers
* **Form & Function:** Fuel is too volatile and viscous to pump up from the ground through dust-fouled electric pumps. It is held in elevated, corrugated iron gravity towers positioned along street meridians.
* **The Interaction:** The driver aligns their cab’s intake cowl beneath a hanging, counterweighted canvas-sheathed fuel sleeve ending in a heavy bronze quarter-turn dry-break nozzle. 
* **World Detail:** Drivers yank a hanging overhead pull-chain to crack the valve. At eye level stands a thick glass metering cylinder with an illuminated internal float bobber, showing the bubbling, sulfur-colored Sol-fuel draining mechanically down into the tank by sheer head-pressure. A mechanical counter clicks off liters with the dry rattle of a typewriter.

### 5. Production Translation Consult (2026-10-05, gemini-3.8-flash)

- Three costliest traps: inverted-hull outlines on thin/lattice geometry; real-time specular/reflection; alpha-blend transparency.
- Three cheapest tricks: alpha-scissor silhouette cards (outline pass disabled); vertex-color baked lighting; vertical framing occlusion (low eaves / deck soffits hiding the fog clip plane).
- Kamome survives cleanly; Tenjin survives best (brutalism = modular kits); Chidori survives with an identity shift (soaring canyon → ground-floor bazaar, fog caps verticality); Kotobuki's cobalt welding-arc signature CAN'T survive the palette ban — retuned to a white-hot AMBER flicker (art-direction call to confirm).
