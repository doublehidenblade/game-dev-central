### 1. Showa-Metabolist Visual Markers

*   **P-Con Form-Tie Indentations:** Unfilled, mathematically gridded circular dimples across board-formed white concrete, accented by authentic wood-grain texture and subtle vertical calcification/rain streaks.
*   **The Skeleton/Pod Cleavage:** A legible, non-monolithic hierarchy: massive vertical slip-formed mega-cores (stairwells/elevators) flanked by cantilevered bracket-seats where modular pods sit bolted via visible, unpainted high-tensile steel mounting plates.
*   **Portholes & Rubber Gaskets:** Deeply recessed windows—either stark circular cuts or squircle pods—sealed with thick, marine-grade vulcanized black neoprene gaskets rather than flush curtain glass.
*   **Expo-Era Space-Frame Trusses:** Tetrahedral ball-joint space frames (Tomoegawa/Mero type) bridging towers or anchoring public plazas, painted industrial safety orange, pale gray, or faded cream.
*   **Exposed External MEP Risers:** Spiral-welded galvanized ventilation conduits, clamped cast-iron wastewater risers, and runs of corrugated steel utility trays stepping up core exteriors, affixed with raw unistrut brackets.
*   **Permanently Mounted Gantry Cranes:** Yellow-and-white rail-mounted derrick cranes crowning the primary slip-cores, implying continuous maintenance, pod extraction, and lifecycle replacement.
*   **Heavy Brise-Soleil Concrete Fins:** Thick, angular cast-concrete louvers shading living modules; heavy structural draft angles rather than razor-thin aerodynamic sheet metal.
*   **Expanded Metal Catwalks:** Galvanized mesh servicing walkways with industrial angle-iron kickplates, double-pipe handrails, and external maintenance cage-ladders running between mega-columns.

### 2. Cliffside Luxury Estates: Showa Modernism vs. The West

**Architectural Language:**
*   Emulate Antonin Raymond, Junzo Sakakura, and Isozaki’s brutalist-residential phase: low-slung horizontal cantilevered slabs of bush-hammered concrete mixed with dark, creosote-stained Japanese cypress (*hinoki*) or charred cedar (*shou sugi ban*).
*   Roofs must utilize matte, unglazed charcoal/silver smoked clay tiles (*ibushi-kawara*) with sharp copper valley-flashing that patinates turquoise, or crisp flat roofs with gravel ballast and cast-concrete coping stones. 
*   Glazing is modular, framed in dark bronze-anodized extruded aluminum, mimicking the proportions of sliding *shoji/fusuma* screens rather than sweeping panoramic frameless glass.

**Vegetation & Hardscaping:**
*   **Belongs:** Wind-sheared Japanese Black Pine (*kuromatsu*) wired into sharp, horizontal silhouettes; dwarf sago cycads (*Cycas revoluta*); wet river-stone courtyards; dry landscape (*karesansui*) raked gravel; cyclopean dry-stacked volcanic stone retaining walls (*ishigaki*); shallow, dark-bottomed reflecting basins spilling over slit-weirs.
*   **Betrays:** Terracotta "Spanish" barrel tiles, smooth white Mediterranean stucco, kidney-shaped swimming pools, bougainvillea, and tall Mexican fan palms.

### 3. Elevated Road Topology: 80s Japanese Civil Engineering

*   **Pylons & Bents:** Monolithic reinforced-concrete hammerhead bents or inverted-U portal frames. They feature chunky chamfered corners, visible horizontal construction joints (lift lines), and cast-in registration numbers in black stencil. 
*   **Guardrails & Acoustic Barriers:** Dual-tier corrugated galvanized steel guardrails painted traffic-safe jade/mint green or off-white, paired with mint-green noise-barrier walls fitted with scratch-mottled acrylic observation windows.
*   **Expansion Joints & Drainage:** Industrial finger-plate (tooth-comb) cast-steel expansion joints across the deck. Cast-iron drop-scupper drains lead to exterior PVC downspouts strapped along the pier faces with steel pipe-clamps.
*   **Signage & Gantry Frameworks:** Heavy, pale-gray angle-iron lattice gantries spanning the viaduct. Signboards are reflective deep forest-green aluminum featuring rounded white borders, high-contrast blocky Showa-era phototypeset Gothic kanji dominant over small English subtext, lit by overhanging tubular incandescent fixtures.
*   **Deck Undersides:** Box-girder concrete undersides routed with exposed, bracketed utility conduits, inspection runways (perforated sheet-metal grating), and yellow clearance warning bars suspended by steel turnbuckles.
*   **Lighting:** Classic curved cobra-head high-pressure sodium fixtures with teardrop glass housings, casting an amber wash across worn asphalt detailed with tar-snake crack repairs and faded white/yellow thermoplastic road striping.
